## Full description

Monday was supposed to be a routine check-up.

**EVERYTHING IS FINE** is a short, story-rich pixel-art game based on the day my daughter was born — and the hours in which my wife almost died while everyone kept saying that everything was fine.

You cannot fight. You cannot fix the hospital. You can hold her hand, bring water, ask questions, keep notes, call a phone that does not answer, and wait outside closed doors.

The less you know, the longer the hospital becomes.

### Features

- A complete narrative experience designed to be played in one sitting
- Exploration, observation, dialogue, phone calls, and a notebook that records contradictions
- No combat, fail states, or conventional puzzles
- Hand-directed top-down pixel-art presentation
- Scene-specific music and atmospheric sound
- Keyboard and touchscreen controls
- Automatic checkpoints

### Controls

- **Move:** Arrow keys or WASD
- **Interact / continue:** E
- **Continue dialogue:** Space
- **Notebook:** N
- **Call:** C, when available
- **Help:** H
- **Fullscreen:** F

Touch controls are available on supported mobile devices.

### Content notice

This game deals with childbirth complications, medical trauma, postpartum haemorrhage, blood transfusion, separation from a newborn, and neonatal intensive care. These subjects are presented through dialogue and pixel-art environments; there is no graphic violence or visible blood.
